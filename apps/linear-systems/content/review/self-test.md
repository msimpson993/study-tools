**How to use this page.** Work each question on paper before you look at the choices; the distractors are the usual mistakes (a missing delay, a wrong ROC side, the simple-pole residue on a double pole, a sign in the costate equation). A wrong answer explains itself and links to the walkthrough that does the same steps: [part A](#/review/part-a) for Z, Laplace, difference equations and Pontryagin, [part B](#/review/part-b) for realization, controllability/observability and eigen problems.
