# linear-systems

Linear Systems Analysis: what a linear system is, and how to predict what it does to any input. Two question maps (Big picture 15 boxes, Full detail; 52 boxes and 55 lines in all), 30 deep dives, 28 live figures drawn on two running examples (an earbud speaker and a car on its suspension), earbud sounds, 101 self-check questions, 4 review pages and a 107-term glossary. Optimal control and feedback appear as applications. Content in `content/`, figures in `src/figs/` (`ex.ts` and `../../packages/core/src/carModel.ts` hold the tested models). Guard: `expect:` in `subject.yaml`.

**Six-domains board** (`src/board/`, folded in from the retired transforms page): nine transform arrows with forward and inverse pair views, draggable poles, 21 examples in 5 tabs (`content/examples.yaml`; the 20 originals plus an Earbud preset drawn in ms and kHz), 9 windows, ZOH/FOH/sinc. `src/board/model.ts` is its unit-tested signal model (`test/board-model.test.ts`). Deep link: `#/board?pair=<ft|lap|zt|dtft|dft|fs|samp|fsamp|map>&preset=<example id>`; boxes 7 and 8, the Fourier-series, sampling, DTFT, DFT and windowing boxes, and the matching dives link into it. `/transforms/` redirects here (see `scripts/build-all.ts`).
