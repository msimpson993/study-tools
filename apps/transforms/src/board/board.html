How the domains relate

Discrete ⇔ periodic

Sampling in one domain makes the other periodic. Sampling time every T makes the spectrum repeat every fs (DTFT). Sampling frequency every Δf = fs/N makes time repeat every NT (DFT and Fourier series). The DFT is discrete and periodic in both.

Slices of the complex planes

The Fourier transform is the Laplace transform on the jω axis. The DTFT is the Z-transform on the unit circle. If that contour is outside the region of convergence (σ ≤ 0 here), the FT/DTFT doesn't exist, but X(s) and X(z) still do.

z = esT ties the bottom row

The left half-plane maps inside the unit circle, the jω axis maps onto it, and ω = ±π/T lands on z = −1. The map is many-to-one: every horizontal strip 2π/T tall covers the whole z-plane. That is aliasing, seen as a pole picture.
